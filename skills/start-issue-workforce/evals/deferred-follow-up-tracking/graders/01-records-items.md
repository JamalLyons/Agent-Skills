The trace records each item when deferral is decided, rather than only in the final response.
Each ledger entry has an identity, problem, file/symbol, discovery source, evidenced base status,
and deferral reason. It retains tracking evidence, recommendations, source PR, questions and
outcomes. All deferred findings are included without expanding #101's scope or correction budget.
